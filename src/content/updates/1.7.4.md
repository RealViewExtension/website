title: "Update notice card on Studio"
---

- After an update, a small card appears in the corner of Studio once, lists what changed, and slides away after a few seconds or when you close it.
- Closing that card clears the red 1 on the toolbar icon, the same as opening the popup does.
- A new "Update notice on Studio" switch in the popup turns the card off; the badge on the icon still appears either way.
